Data-quality limitations: molecules rated POOR cannot be constrained by these data regardless of other numbers; LIMITED ratings widen the credible intervals' interpretation. See Section 3 for the per-molecule ratings.

Model assumptions and limits: the retrieval assumes an isothermal atmosphere, constant-with-altitude mixing ratios, a grey cloud deck, and fixed mass, gravity and stellar radius from the catalog. There is no stellar-contamination treatment. Results are conditional on these assumptions and on the stated priors; the retrieval is not independent of them. The forward opacities used here are documented in Section 6.

9. Visualization note (3D scientific digital twin)

The dashboard twin at /twin/{{ ctx.analysis_id }} renders the measured, inferred, derived and assumed parameters of this analysis as a star-planet-orbit scene with a uniform atmosphere shell. It shows no surfaces, continents, oceans, spatial molecule maps, or photographs; the atmosphere shell thickness is visually exaggerated and labelled not to scale. The twin is an illustration of the tabulated parameters, not an independent measurement.
